Quick tips

📁  Everything lives in folders — tap "+ New folder" on the home screen to make one.
⭐  Tap the star on any photo to pin it to Favorites.
🔒  The buttons at the top have backup / restore, dark mode and colours. The gear icon has auto-lock and a lock for individual folders.
🗑️  Deleted photos sit safely in Trash for 30 days before they're gone for good.
