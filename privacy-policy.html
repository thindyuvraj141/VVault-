Privacy Policy — V Vault

Last updated: 22 September 2026

V Vault ("the app") is a personal photo and file vault that lets you lock and hide files behind a password or PIN, on your own device. This page explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

1. What V Vault stores

All vault content (photos, files, folders, and their names) is stored locally on your device, inside your browser's storage (IndexedDB). V Vault does not have its own server, and does not send your vault content anywhere unless you explicitly choose to back it up.

2. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, V Vault uses Google's sign-in to create an encrypted backup file of your vault and save it to your Google Drive. This feature:

  • Only requests the drive.file scope, which lets the app create and access only the files it creates itself — it cannot see, read, or modify any other file in your Google Drive.
  • Encrypts the backup with your vault password before uploading, using AES-GCM encryption, entirely in your browser. Google (and V Vault's developer) cannot read the contents of your vault.
  • Is entirely optional. If you don't enable it, no data ever leaves your device.

3. What we don't do

  • We do not run our own servers that store your vault data.
  • We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not access your Google account information beyond what's needed to sign you in and save the backup file you create.

4. Your control over your data

You can delete your local vault at any time from within the app. You can delete any backup files from your Google Drive directly, or revoke V Vault's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out via the contact details listed on the app's GitHub page.
