Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how V Vault ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. V Vault is designed as a local, private photo and video vault, and we deliberately collect and store as little data as possible.

1. Information we do not collect

V Vault does not operate its own servers to store your photos, videos, passwords, or PINs. All media you add to the vault is stored locally on your device, encrypted with a password or PIN that you set. We cannot see, access, or recover your vault contents, and we do not have the ability to reset a forgotten password or PIN.

2. Google Account and Google Drive access

If you choose to enable the optional backup feature, V Vault requests limited access to your Google account for the following purposes only:

  • To create a single, dedicated backup folder in your own Google Drive
  • To upload an encrypted copy of your vault to that folder, at your request
  • To list and restore encrypted backup files from that same folder, at your request
  • To display your Google account name, email address, and profile picture within the app so you know which account is connected

V Vault does not access any other files, folders, photos, or documents in your Google Drive. Your backup file is encrypted before it is uploaded, and V Vault does not have server-side access to it — only your device, using your password or PIN, can decrypt it.

3. Local device permissions

V Vault may request access to your device's photo library, camera, or biometric sensor (such as fingerprint or face unlock) solely to let you import media into the vault or unlock the app. This data is processed locally and is never transmitted to us.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or vault contents with any third party. We do not use your data for advertising.

5. Data retention and deletion

Vault contents remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Backups stored in your Google Drive remain there until you delete them yourself from within the app or directly through Google Drive.

6. Children's privacy

V Vault is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at thindyuvraj141@gmail.com.
