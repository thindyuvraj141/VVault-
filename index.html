V Vault

Keep your private photos and videos truly private

V Vault is a personal photo and video vault app. It lets you lock away sensitive media behind a password or PIN, organize it into private folders, and optionally back it up securely to your own Google Drive — all without anyone else being able to see it.

What V Vault does

  • Locks your photos and videos behind a password, PIN, or biometric unlock
  • Organizes hidden media into private, named folders
  • Lets you optionally back up your encrypted vault to your own Google Drive account
  • Restores your vault from a previous Google Drive backup on a new device
  • Works entirely on your device — your media is never sent to any V Vault server

Why V Vault asks for Google Drive access

If you choose to enable backups, V Vault uses your Google account only to create a dedicated backup folder in your own Google Drive and to upload or restore your encrypted vault file. V Vault never accesses, reads, or shares any other files in your Google Drive, and no data is ever sent to third parties.
